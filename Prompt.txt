🧠 PROJECT NAME

Build a scalable beauty marketplace platform called
Parloora 

Parloora is an online marketplace specializing in beauty and wellness service bookings. It connects customers with local beauty parlors, salons, and spas through a digital platform, simplifying appointment scheduling and service discovery. The company serves as a bridge between service providers and clients in Bangladesh’s rapidly digitizing personal care industry.

Tagline: Book your selfcare services in seconds
🎯 OBJECTIVE

Create a high-performance, mobile-first platform that includes:

• Customer Website
• Mobile App (Android + iOS)
• Parlour Owner Dashboard
• Super Admin Panel

The system must be:
• Fast
• Secure
• Scalable
• SEO optimized
• Easy to manage from admin
